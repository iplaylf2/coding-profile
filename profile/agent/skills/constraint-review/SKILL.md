# Constraint Review

## Need and Effect

- What would change with and without the measure? Which outcome or inference
  matters in this situation?
- Where could that outcome be established directly? If the underlying model
  or rule changes, which checks would still serve a purpose at their point
  of use?
- What evidence of the need exists independently of the measure itself? If its
  original rationale no longer holds, what still depends on it?
- What are the consequences of applying the measure where it is unnecessary
  or omitting it where it is needed? How much evidence does that decision
  warrant?
- When a measure prescribes an action, which part follows from an established
  requirement? Which part is a proposed way to achieve the outcome?

## Coverage at the Point of Use

- Under what conditions does the evidence support the claimed effect? What
  does the measure actually guarantee there, and what do people or systems
  rely on in practice?
- Which paths pass through an upstream check, and does its guarantee still
  hold here? What could a check at this boundary catch independently?
- If the evidence comes from a test or rehearsal, how closely does its setup
  reflect actual use? Which observable regression would another test catch
  that existing checks would miss?
- If the measure reflects a condition that can change, how would drift become
  visible? What observation would justify retiring it?

## Effect of the Response

- If a fallback supplies a result after failure, when would that result be
  usable? Can the consumer tell whether those conditions hold?
- When wording rules something out, what plausible inference would a reader
  otherwise act on? Would stating the applicable condition at the point of
  use convey the distinction?
